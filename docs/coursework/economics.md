# Venture-economics snapshot — assumptions, not market evidence

Free product: browser-based group assignment planner.
Proposed paid offer: a human reviews task scope, assignments, and milestones, then returns a finalized plan. Not currently sold or fulfilled through the prototype.

| Item                                     | Working assumption                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| User                                     | Student coordinating a group assignment due within seven days, with unassigned work         |
| Buyer / payer                            | The coordinating student pays for a human review before the team divides the remaining work |
| Price                                    | $5 per team per assignment; no subscription                                                 |
| Delivery labor                           | 10 minutes at an assumed $15/hour = $2.50                                                   |
| Payment processing allowance             | $0.50 per sale; hypothetical budget, not a provider quote                                   |
| Other variable support/hosting allowance | $0.25 per sale                                                                              |
| Total variable cost                      | $3.25 per order                                                                             |
| Contribution before acquisition          | $1.75 per order (35%)                                                                       |
| Acquisition                              | Student referrals and class/club outreach where permitted                                   |
| Acquisition time allowance               | 5 minutes per acquired team at $15/hour = $1.25                                             |
| Contribution after acquisition allowance | $0.50 per first order                                                                       |
| Repeat assumption                        | 2 orders per team per semester; untested                                                    |
| Fixed startup labor                      | Illustrative 2 hours at $15/hour = $30; replace with actual time                            |
| Break-even                               | 60 first orders at $0.50 contribution to recover $30, before taxes and other fixed costs    |

Sensitivity: if review takes 20 minutes, labor becomes $5, total variable cost $5.75, and contribution is negative $0.75 before acquisition. If no customers pay, revenue is zero regardless of planner usage.

Most fragile assumptions: students will pay for planning when free alternatives exist; review can be delivered well in 10 minutes; outreach converts at five minutes per paying team. The current usability/adoption test does not validate these. Next economic test: a clearly disclosed paid concierge offer with actual fulfillment, after instructor guidance and appropriate consent. No actual sales or payment evidence exists.

## Cash implication and evidence boundary

No sales, commitments, or payment tests exist. Price and labor/processing/support estimates are planning assumptions. The customer and acquisition channel are hypotheses. This snapshot is not tied to actual demand evidence yet.

If the founder supplies the labor without a cash salary, a hypothetical $5 order leaves $4.25 after $0.50 processing and a $0.25 support/hosting reserve. Delivery plus acquisition labor consumes 15 minutes, valued at $3.75, leaving $0.50 economic contribution. Labor is an opportunity cost in that scenario, not a cash payment. If hired out, that labor becomes a cash expense. Tax and other fixed cash costs are excluded.

Illustrative five-order scenario: revenue $25, processing/reserve $3.75, cash before founder pay $21.25, founder labor opportunity cost $18.75, contribution $2.50 before startup labor. After the illustrative $30 startup labor allowance, economic result is negative $27.50. This is a scenario, not actual revenue.

No paid tooling or paid hosting was purchased for this build. Existing account charges, if any, are not measured. Use actual invoices and provider terms before launching payments.
