      Built for the moment
      after “let’s make a group chat.”

      Fairshare is for the student coordinating a group assignment due within a week. It turns a list of work into a draft everyone can review before getting started.

      How does the planning work?

      The largest task goes to the teammate with the lowest assigned effort, then the process repeats. Dates follow your task order, with a final day for review when time allows. Equal estimated hours do not guarantee equal difficulty. You can edit every owner and date. Prefer not to assign people? Choose “Let teammates claim tasks,” share the team link, and balance whatever is left over in one click.

      Who would pay for this?

      The basic planner stays free for anyone. We are testing whether instructors or departments will pay $150 per course section per semester for a team version every student in the course can use, starting with paid Spring 2027 pilot sections. We are also testing a $3 one-time price per project for students. In our first check-in, 0 of 3 student coordinators said they would pay $15 a semester. Instructor willingness to pay is untested.

      What happens to my information?

      No accounts, analytics, or server storage. Plans are saved only in this browser, and you can delete them anytime. A team link holds the plan after the # in the address, which browsers do not send to any server. Anyone with the link can read the plan, so use initials or aliases and avoid sensitive information.

      What are we testing?

      This student project studies whether a draft plan helps students coordinate real group assignments. Participation in any study is voluntary. No analytics are collected by this website. Results mentioned here come from a small, self-reported check-in.
