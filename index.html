Fairshare is for the student coordinating a group assignment due within a week. It turns a list of work into a draft everyone can review before getting started.

How does the planning work?

The largest task goes to the teammate with the lowest assigned effort, then the process repeats. Dates follow your task order, with a final day for review when time allows. Equal estimated hours do not guarantee equal difficulty. You can edit every owner and date.

Who would pay for this?

The planner is free. Our proposed next offer is a human review of the plan for $5 per team per assignment, paid by one team member. That service is not available yet. The price and willingness to pay are untested.

What happens to my information?

No accounts, analytics, or server storage. Your entries remain in this tab until you copy or download them. Use initials or aliases and avoid sensitive information. Closing or refreshing the page clears your work.

What are we testing?

Our first test asks whether 2 of 3 students with an assignment due within a week can independently build and review a plan, then voluntarily put it into their actual team chat or shared document within five minutes. This is a small exploratory test. No outcome is claimed before actual participant testing.
